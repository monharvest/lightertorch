Battery life depends on the type of batteries used and how the torch is made. Some torches use rechargeable batteries, while others need regular replacements.

We tested many torches to find the best ones. Our goal was to find torches that are very bright and have long-lasting batteries. We looked at how well they worked in different situations and how easy they were to use. This helped us pick the best torches for different needs.

<h2>Buying Guide</h2>
Choosing the right torch can be tricky. We've put together some key things to look for when shopping for a bright, long-lasting torch.
<h3>Brightness</h3>
Look for the lumen rating. Higher lumens mean a brighter light. For general use, 100-300 lumens works well. For outdoor adventures, consider 1000+ lumens.
<h3>Battery Life</h3>
Check the runtime specs. Longer is better, especially for camping or emergencies. Some torches can last 50+ hours on low settings.
<h3>Beam Distance</h3>
This tells you how far the light reaches. Longer distances are great for outdoor use. Shorter beams work fine for around the house.
<h3>Modes</h3>
Multiple brightness modes let you adjust the light. Low modes save battery. High modes give maximum visibility when needed.
<h3>Water Resistance</h3>
An IPX4 rating or higher protects against rain and splashes. This is important for outdoor use.
<h3>Size and Weight</h3>
Consider how you'll use the torch. Compact models are easy to carry. Larger ones may offer more power and features.
<h3>Materials</h3>
Look for durable materials like aluminum. Plastic can work for casual use but may not hold up as well.
<h3>Extra Features</h3>
Some handy extras to consider:
<ul>
 	<li>Adjustable focus</li>
 	<li>Clip or lanyard for easy carrying</li>
 	<li>Multiple light colors</li>
 	<li>USB rechargeable battery</li>
</ul>
By keeping these factors in mind, we can find a torch that fits our needs perfectly.
