<h2 class="wp-block-heading">A flashlight's performance is best perceived by its lumens </h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><br>A flashlight's performance is best perceived by its lumens which is the measurement of the<br>utmost light output from a torch. A good flashlight however has around 200-500 lumens<br>which is suitable for all indoor and outdoor tasks. Even when going camping, hunting and<br>searching, flashlights which retain <strong>around 200-500 lumens</strong> power function as the fairest.<br>A misconception that has taken over the flashlight market is that the greater the lumens the<br>more promising the flashlight. However, this is not something entirely true. The performance<br>of a flashlight also vastly depends on some other aspects that shouldn't be ignored at the<br>time of buying it. Like beam distance, running time, battery etc. So don't exclusively rely on<br>the lumens but treat them as a significant factor like many others.<br>A thing to keep in mind:<br>Moreover, for a flashlight, the more lumens that you have, the farther your light is going to<br>travel, you are going to achieve more focus and an alienated view. However, one thing<br>needs to be placed in mind when dealing with lumens primarily is, the more lumens wielded<br>in a flashlight the shorter its battery life. This will make you realise to go for something with a<br>reasonable lumens amount and an adequate battery life if you don't have anything serious to<br>do. This means, the job that you intend to perform with the flashlight will decide how many<br>lumens you will really need.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>FMU LED Tactical Flashlight</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you are involved in search Operations, and rescue, you will obviously need surplus lumens<br>rather than a flashlight that is just going to be used indoors for minor purposes when the<br>power goes out. However, if you have no such use and need for little chores indoors and<br>outdoors your lumen need will automatically decrease unless you are a flash-a-holic and<br>need something powerful on your hands all the time.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So let's dive into a few different situations that you would want a flashlight for and decide the<br>decent lumen count you’re going to need in it. From there it’s up to you to decipher in which<br>situation, which lumen count will work as the best for you.<br></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Casual Indoor And Outdoor Chores:</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><br>Flashlights within 100-200 lumens are illustrative for all indoor uses and some outdoor<br>activities too. Like looking up in your yard at night for something or going to check in your<br>cycle that stands outside in a poor lit environment. An average flashlight which emits around<br>the same lumen count of 100 to 200, will be a decent choice for urban and even suburban<br>areas. These allow you to search for things within a 20-30 feet range that's not very much<br>but ideal according to the circumstances.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Searching Activities:</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><br>A flashlight rendering a 200-400 lumen count is ideal for situations where you are stepping<br>out of well lit areas. It might be for searching in woods, going camping or cycling through<br>woods. It works well for searching purposes. It perfectly fits to searching activities within a 50<br>meter distance. These can even lighten up a small room well, and can be used as a bicycle<br>headlight too with it's powerful beam.<br></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Professional Use:</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><br>Professionally applicable flashlights are the ones that are used by engineers, mechanics,<br>plumbers and even police men. These flashlights are included in the most powerful<br>flashlights category as the lumen count reaches up to 500-1000 lumens. They can reach<br>farfetch areas and can easily blind the attacker or any person that looks directly into them.<br>This lumen count means you are entering into a very capable flashlight for a general use<br>zone. These flashlights have an output which is efficient but pretty cost effective.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>So prefer going for a flashlight that fits your most recent job and don't get too excited over<br>lumens as every job requires a sufficient amount of light and more than that seems useless.</p>
