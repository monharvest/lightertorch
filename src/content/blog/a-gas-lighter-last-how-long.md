<p><strong>How long does a Bic lighter last?</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When it comes to determining <strong>how long does a lighter last? </strong>Bic lighters are guaranteed to top the list with their durability. It's not uncommon for a Bic lighter to take action after months or even years after use, even if you had forgotten about their existence. They are available in a mini and regular size, but the mini versions are said to be better than the latter as they are easily replaceable. It has been claimed to last for about 3000 lights but can go far more than that number. Also, the regular Bic lighter can continually burn for about an hour, while the mini version can burn for 20 minutes. While the mini lighters burn for less time, they are easily refillable, and the flints can be replaced.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>How long does a zippo lighter last?</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A zippo lighter is said to keep its fuel supply intact for about two to three weeks easily; however, at most times, the fuel evaporates within two weeks maximum. If compared to a Bic lighter, a zippo lighter will only last for about 200 lights, while a Bic lighter can last for years. At the same time, zippo lighters are vintage known for their adaptability, durability, and repairability elements. While zippo leaks its fuel to evaporate at lightning speed, it can function with various fuel sources such as gasoline, kerosene, or diesel.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>How long does a butane lighter last?</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Butane answers the question of <strong>how long does a gas lighter lasts</strong> with an average of 6 months. Butane lighters are amazing for everyday use to light fires on several occasions. Their long-lasting capacity can prove to be helpful in various situations, so if you haven't invested in one, it would be great to do it now. You can easily refill the lighters with a butane gas can which keeps elevating their life span.&nbsp;</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Things to do to make your lighter last long:</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It would help if you kept in mind a few important factors while dealing with a gas lighter, and the answer to <strong>how long does a gas lighter last</strong> will be in your own hands. Try to implement these points, and you'll witness the difference yourself:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li><strong>Warm-up</strong></li></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Heating up the liquid inside your lighter before it loses pressure will allow you to get more use out of it. Keeping it warm will escalate its pressure and increase its lifespan. It just prevents fuel loss. If your lighter has liquid inside of it but has lost the warmth and pressure, it won't light. So, keep it warm to build pressure to make them last longer.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li><strong>Store it in a safe and cool place</strong></li></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Storing the lighter is one of the most common questions asked. If you want your lighter to last long, store it in a cool place. While it may counter the keeping it warm statement, you need to keep it in a cool place when it's not in use. If you're going on a trip, store it in a freezer to prevent the fuel from evaporating and helping the lighter last long.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol><li><strong>Use a pressurized jar</strong></li></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Storing your lighter in a cool, pressurized jar will also help it last longer than usual. It will prevent the fuel from leaking and evaporating and make it live longer. Whether you're using a Bic or a zippo, these particular ways of storage are guaranteed to help your gas lighter last longer.&nbsp;</p>
